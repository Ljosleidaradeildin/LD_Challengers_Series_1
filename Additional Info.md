### **2. Format**

- Open Qualifiers:
    - 20 spots available. First come, first served open signups through form.
    - Stage 1: Single-elimination BO3 knockout. Stage 2: GSL Groups
    - 2 teams from each GSL group advance to the Main Stage. 8 Teams in total.
- Main Stage:
    - Single Elimination Bracket
    - All Games BO3
    - 8 Teams Total
    - Winners of Semi Finals Qualify to LAN Finals
    - Losers of Semi Finals play Third Place Decider
- LAN Final:
    - Single Best of 5

### **3. Teams**

- 8 Teams from Open Qualifiers

### **4. Eligibility**

This tournament is solely for teams composing of 5/5 players that are citizens or residents of Iceland.

### **5. Qualification**

Upon signing up to a qualifier, the five players selected as the starting lineup will be considered the Primary Team if the team qualifies.

If a team requires a stand-in for a qualifier, the stand-in must be registered as one of the team’s two substitutes and must not be listed among the starting five players.
